How Financial Cycles & Months Work
1. Monthly Budget Cycle

Budgets are tied to a Calendar Month (YYYY-MM). Any transaction with a date in October falls into October's budget limit. On November 1st, spending resets to ₹0 for the new monthly cycle.

2. Recurring Payment Cycle

Subscriptions (Netflix, Rent) have a Next Due Date. When that date arrives, processing it logs a Debit and automatically moves the due date forward (e.g. +1 month via ADD_MONTHS).

3. Forecast Moving Cycle

The prediction system calculates a 3-Month Rolling Average. When September ends, it uses July, August, and September's actual spending to project next month's numbers.
