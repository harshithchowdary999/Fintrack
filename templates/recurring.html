How Monthly Billing Cycles Work in FinTrack
1 Calendar Anchor

Each subscription is anchored to a specific calendar day (e.g. 1st for Rent, 11th for Broadband). The schedule tracks renewals automatically within the active monthly cycle.

2 PL/SQL Cursor Engine

Clicking Process Due Payments invokes Oracle stored procedure generate_recurring_transactions. An explicit cursor scans all due items and logs debit transactions automatically.

3 Cycle Advancement

Once processed, Oracle advances next_due_date by one full cycle (ADD_MONTHS(date, 1)). Account balances update in real-time via the DML trigger trg_update_account_balance.
